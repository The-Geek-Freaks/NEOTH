# Code of Conduct

NEOTH follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Summary

- Be respectful in issues, PRs, and discussions.
- Disagree on technical grounds, not on people.
- No personal attacks, no discrimination, no spam.
- Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors who repeatedly violate the spirit of this document.

## Reporting

If something feels off, email the maintainers listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled in confidence.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — GitHub repo, Discord, mailing list, conferences where NEOTH is represented.

## Enforcement

Maintainers handle reports privately. Outcomes range from a private warning to a permanent ban from project spaces. Decisions are final but may be revisited if new information emerges.
